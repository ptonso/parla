## facts

- No custom slide macros are assumed. Use standard Beamer: `frame`, `block`, `columns`, `itemize` and `\includegraphics`.
- A chapter becomes a divider frame, `\begin{frame}[plain]\centering{\Large\textbf{<heading>}}\\[0.6em]{\normalsize <subtitle>}\end{frame}`. Every chapter gets a divider frame, to keep the narrative spine.
