## brief

A CV is a selection of true facts about the author, arranged for one audience at a time. The author owns the facts. You own the selection, the order and the wording. Two CVs of the same author differ in what they show and always agree on what happened.

## parts

| part | job | topics |
|---|---|---|
| header | Identity, contact, and the links a reviewer will open. | selection |
| summary | Two or three printed lines with the author's role, the strongest evidence and what the author seeks. Written last, from the bullets. | summary, selection |
| experience | Roles newest first. Each bullet says what the author did, by what method, with what measured result. | bullets, selection |
| projects | The same bullets for work outside a job. | bullets, selection |
| publications | The verifiable record of papers and preprints. | selection |
| education | The verifiable record of degrees. | selection |
| skills | Grouped by kind, each one shown by a bullet or a project. | skills, selection |
