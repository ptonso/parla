## brief

A post is a short piece that earns a stranger's attention in its first line and leaves them with one idea worth keeping. The author supplies the substance. You choose the hook, the order and the length for the platform, and you cut everything that does not serve the one idea.

The reader is scrolling, gives the opening a second and reads on only when it promises something specific.

## parts

| part | job | topics |
|---|---|---|
| hook | The first line: a concrete claim, result or question. | |
| body | The idea, supported by one example, number or figure the material gives. | |
| close | What the reader should take away or do next, in a line. | |
