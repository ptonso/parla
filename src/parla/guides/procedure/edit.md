Goal: change what the author asked for and leave the rest as it was.

Read the built output and its diff first, as a reviewer would: does this sentence say what the evidence supports, does this transition work, does this paragraph belong here. Protect citations, specific technical claims, mathematical logic, named architectures and the exact scope of a cited proposition, and keep their meaning exact. Syntax, transitions, generic verbs and paragraph rhythm are free to change within the reach the phase allows. Keep the author's wording where it still fits, reflect every point of the feedback, and leave existing figure and table environments and their references as they are. As the text matures, make the smallest edit that does the job, and propose any change that reshapes the artifact before making it.
