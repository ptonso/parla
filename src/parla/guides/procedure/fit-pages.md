procedure: fit-pages
---
Goal: bring the artifact to its page target by cutting content.

Measure with `parla tool measure`, and state the cut as a percentage of what you have. You see the whole document, so look for redundancy across sections first: keep motivation, background and restated results where the reader first needs them and remove them elsewhere. Then assume every section can lose a whole paragraph without losing its central claim, and find it before trimming sentences. Tightening wording rarely moves the page count, so be able to point to a paragraph or worked example you removed. Keep every section in its order, even when one shrinks to its central claim. Measure again, and report each cut or merge with its reason so the author can review what changed.
