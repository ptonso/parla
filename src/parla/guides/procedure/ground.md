Goal: every citation supports what the text says it supports.

List the citations with `parla tool claims <artifact>`. For each one, state the explicit claims it backs (the clause the citation covers, split into atomic claims, at the exact wording of metrics and scope). Read the cited paper with `parla tool source <citekey>` and pick the passages that are direct evidence, since loose topical overlap proves nothing. Give each claim a verdict: grounded when the evidence supports it as written, partially grounded when a material qualifier, metric, scope or strength is unbacked, ungrounded otherwise. Narrow or remove what the evidence does not support, editing only the affected clause and keeping the paragraph's argument, and keep the text free of any remark about evidence or grounding. Report each change to the author.
