---
kind: procedure
procedure: read-figure
---
Goal: describe a figure so that a reader can reconstruct what it shows without seeing it.

This holds for any type: a plot, a multi-panel plot, a dashboard, a diagram, a table-like visual or a screenshot. Ground every statement in content visible in the image, and mark what is absent or unreadable with a short explicit note. Transcribe the text in the image faithfully, with its symbols, units, percentages and signs, grouping repeated labels on a dense image into one entry. Keep the description compact, as dense noun phrases.
