procedure: read-paper
---
Goal: report what a cited paper says, from its own text.

Frame the paper from its abstract, introduction and conclusion with `parla tool source <citekey> --section <label>`: its type by what the authors gave the field (methodological, theoretical, empirical, review or perspective), what it establishes as a claim in under 15 words, about three keywords, what a reader must already know, and the idea as you would explain it at a whiteboard (what the authors do instead of the obvious thing, and why that pays off). Then read the body section by section and collect the evidence with its page: problem, contribution, method, results with their numbers, metrics, baselines, ablations and limitations. Keep the three or four items that carry the paper. Write only what the text supports and mark the rest `n/a`. Report the note in the reply, and write it to a file when the author asks.
