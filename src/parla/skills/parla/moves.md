# Moves

A move is a named technique for one request, such as compressing, elaborating, hedging or sharpening. It is loaded for that job only, and the author chooses it.

## Signs a move would help

- a passage over its share of the budget: compress
- an idea folded into a line when the reader needs it laid out: elaborate
- a claim stronger than its evidence: hedge
- hedges on a claim the author states outright: sharpen
- an explanation a newcomer cannot follow: a reader trait or another move

`parla guide list move` lists the moves the author has.

## Offer it as a question

Name the move and say what it would do here: "this section is two pages over, so I could compress it to fit; shall I?". On yes, load it with `parla guide show --move <name>` and apply it within the phase's reach. Without a yes, make the plain edit.
